Approach

Direction first.
Deployment second.

Start narrow. Prove value. Scale what works.

The risk is no longer whether AI works. It is deploying it without a value case, controls or an operating model — and carrying the cost of that across a firm.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    Understand the workflows, the data behind them, the constraints they operate under and the economics they move. Before anything is proposed, we establish what the current state actually costs.

    • Workflow mapping
    • Data and access review
    • Constraints
    • Current-state cost
  2. 02

    Value case

    Every candidate workflow is ranked on economic impact, feasibility, risk, time-to-value and implementation cost. Not every workflow needs AI. This stage is where we say which ones do.

    Illustrative prioritisation — real sequencing comes out of the diagnostic
    WorkflowImpactFeasibilityTime‑to‑value
    IC preparationHighHigh21 days
    DiligenceHighHigh30 days
    Portfolio reportingHighMedium45 days
  3. 03

    Blueprint

    The design: systems, integrations, which data is reachable and by whom, where a human signs off, what controls apply, and how the outcome will be measured once it is live.

    • Systems & integrations
    • Data access
    • Human review points
    • Controls
    • Measurement
    • Sequence
  4. 04

    Deploy & measure

    Build the system, integrate it with what the firm already runs, then measure the outcome against the value case. What holds up gets extended. What does not gets stopped.

    • Build
    • Integrate
    • Measure
    • Extend or stop

Operating model

Judgment stays human.

ACE systems prepare, analyse and monitor. They do not decide. Every consequential output passes a review gate before it becomes a firm position.

Assurance

Your data stays controlled.

Permissions, retention, review and auditability are designed into every deployment rather than added once the system is already live.

What enters the system?

Data control

Scoped per deployment. Only the sources a workflow genuinely needs are connected, and that scope is written down.

Who can see what?

Access

Permissions follow the firm's existing structure. A system does not widen who can reach a document.

Where is approval required?

Human review

Review gates are placed during design, not retrofitted. Consequential output does not leave the system unapproved.

What produced this output?

Traceability

Generated work carries its inputs: who, what, when, and which sources it was drawn from.

What is stored, and for how long?

Retention

Retention is a design decision made at blueprint stage and is part of the deployment, not a setting discovered later.

What does it connect to?

Integration

Systems attach to the estate that already exists. Fewer disconnected tools, more connected workflows.

Who can stop a workflow?

Oversight

Named people in your firm can pause, change or withdraw any deployed workflow without going through us first.

On certification: controls are specified per engagement and written into the contract. ACE makes no certification claim it cannot evidence. Where a firm requires a specific standard, we scope to it before work begins rather than after.
